Coco

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

Coco doesn't collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app doesn't send anything over the network.

Your documents

The PDFs and comics you add stay on your iPhone, in Coco's folder in the Files app. Coco reads them only to show them to you. If you use iCloud Drive or another storage provider through the Files app, that's handled by Apple or that provider, not by Coco.

The camera

Vanity Mode asks for access to the camera, because iOS only lets an app use the outer screen while the phone is open when the camera is active. Coco keeps the camera running for that reason alone. It never records, saves, analyses or sends any images. You can turn off camera access at any time in Settings, and everything else in Coco keeps working.

Purchases

Optional outfits and color themes are bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; Coco only receives confirmation of what you own, so it can unlock it. We never see your payment details.

Settings

Your preferences, such as Coco's outfit, the color theme and where you were in each document, are stored on your device only.

Children

Coco collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
