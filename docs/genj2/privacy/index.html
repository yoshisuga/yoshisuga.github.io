GenJ2 Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

In short: GenJ2 does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What GenJ2 collects

Nothing. GenJ2 does not collect personal information, device identifiers, usage data, location, contacts or any other data, and it contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software.

What stays on your device

The games you import, their save states, and your settings are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. We never receive them. If you back up your device to iCloud or your computer, the app's data may be included in that backup under Apple's own terms. Deleting the app removes its data from your device.

Purchases

GenJ2 offers an optional one-time purchase (GenJ2 Premium). Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not see or store your payment details, your Apple ID or your name. The app only asks Apple whether the purchase has been made so it can unlock the features.

Internet and the games you import

GenJ2 itself does not send information about you to us or to anyone else. The games and applications you import are programs made by third parties; if one of them connects to the internet, that connection is made by that program and is governed by its own creator's practices. Only import files you trust.

Motion sensors

If you turn on the phone skin's metallic sheen, GenJ2 reads your device's motion sensors to move the highlight as you tilt the phone. This is used only on the device, at that moment, and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Children

GenJ2 collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Ask on Discord or open an issue on GitHub.
