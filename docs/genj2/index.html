GenJ2

J2ME & iAppli Emulator for iPhone and iPad

GenJ2 plays J2ME (Java ME) games and Japanese iAppli (DoJa) applications on your iPhone or iPad. It comes with four original games so you can try it right away, and you can import your own .jar files.

Need help or found a bug?
Ask on Discord or open an issue on GitHub. Please include your device, iOS version, the app version and the name of the game.

Getting started

  • Import games: tap + in the library and choose a .jar file (or a .jar with its .jam and .sp files for an iAppli, or a whole folder or .zip). You can also use AirDrop, the Files app or the share sheet.
  • Play: tap a game. Use the on-screen keypad or d-pad, and the soft keys under the screen. The ••• button opens the menu: save and load states, settings, phone skin and more.
  • Settings: the gear on the library has app-wide settings. Every game can also have its own (screen size, scaling, smoothing, frame rate, sound, soft key codes).

Frequently asked questions

Where do I get games?
GenJ2 does not include or download any third-party games. Import files you have the right to use.

A game doesn't start, or looks or sounds wrong.
Open the game's settings and try a different screen size, scaling or frame rate. For iAppli games make sure the .jam file (and .sp file, if any) was imported with the .jar. If it still fails, tell us the name of the game on Discord or GitHub.

The soft keys or arrows don't work in a game.
Games from different phone makers expect different key codes. In the game's settings change Soft key codes (Standard, Motorola, Siemens) or turn on Arrows send 8 / 4 / 6 / 2.

What are save states?
Save and restore a game at any moment from the ••• menu. Every game has one free save slot. GenJ2 Premium unlocks 10 slots per game and autosave when you leave the app.

What does Premium include?
A one-time purchase (no subscription): 10 save slots for every game, autosave, and more phone colors and finishes. All games and settings are free.

How do I restore my purchase?
Settings → Premium → Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple ID you used to buy it. It also works on your other devices.

How do I get the retro phone look?
In a game, open ••• → Phone skin (or Settings → Controls) to choose the candy-bar or flip phone, a color, stretched or normal proportions, and the metallic sheen.

Does it work on iPhone Duo?
Yes: folded with the game on one screen and controls on the other, folded in portrait, full-screen landscape and the outer screen.

Does GenJ2 need an internet connection?
No. See the Privacy Policy.
